# Privacy Policy for Hacker News Reader

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

## Who we are

Hacker News Reader is published by Toine. We do not require an account to use the app. For privacy questions or data requests, contact [toto8@duck.com](mailto:toto8@duck.com).

## Information the app handles

### Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, saved article content for offline reading, Reading Sprint lists and session state, reading preferences, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not keep this information on our servers. Local notifications are scheduled by your device.

### Hacker News and article websites

The app requests Hacker News stories and comments directly from the public Hacker News API. When you save an external article for offline reading, the app requests that article directly from its website and stores the extracted content on your device. If you open an article link, your device connects to that website. Those services may receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested URL, and handle it under their own privacy policies. These requests do not pass through our servers.

### Purchases and RevenueCat

On iOS, Apple’s App Store processes subscription payments; on Android, Google Play does. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat provides subscription offerings, purchase and restore processing, and subscription status (entitlements) for the app.

The RevenueCat SDK creates a random, anonymous app user ID because the app has no user account or sign-in. RevenueCat may receive that ID, purchase and subscription transaction information, and technical information about the device and app needed to provide its service. When the Reading Sprint offer is shown, the app also sends RevenueCat a custom paywall impression event identified as `reading_sprint`. We use RevenueCat’s subscription and paywall reporting to operate the premium feature and understand how the offer is used. We do not use this data for cross-app advertising or ad tracking.

See [RevenueCat’s privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy-policy), [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How information is shared

We do not sell personal information or use an advertising network in the app. Information is handled by Apple or Google Play, RevenueCat, Hacker News, and external article websites only as described above or when needed to provide the service, comply with law, or protect the app and its users. Those services process information under their privacy policies and applicable agreements.

## Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Device backups may retain app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

We do not operate user accounts or store bookmarks, preferences, reading sessions, or browsing history on our servers. Apple and Google Play retain store transaction records under their policies and legal requirements. RevenueCat retains subscription-related data as needed to provide its service and under its applicable agreements and retention practices.

To request deletion of data associated with the app’s anonymous RevenueCat ID, email [toto8@duck.com](mailto:toto8@duck.com). We will use the information you provide to identify the relevant record and submit the request to RevenueCat. Store purchase records are managed by Apple or Google Play and cannot be deleted by us. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple or Google Play account settings.

## Your choices

You can turn off Reading Sprint reminders in the app or change notification permissions in your device settings. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. For questions or requests about information handled by RevenueCat, contact us at the email above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on our [privacy policy page](https://hackernewsreaderapp.download/privacy-policy) with its updated date.
